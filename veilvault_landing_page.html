VeilVault is a private AI-powered trading vault protocol that lets users allocate capital to autonomous strategies without exposing their positions or proprietary strategy logic onchain.

01 / PRIVATE

Protect strategy logic

Trading decisions and sensitive portfolio state can remain confidential instead of turning every strategy into public information.

02 / AGENTIC

Let agents act

AI agents can translate defined strategy rules and market signals into autonomous trading decisions within controlled execution boundaries.

03 / VERIFIABLE

Prove the result

Users should be able to verify execution and evaluate performance without requiring the strategy's private logic to be exposed.

A new interface for onchain capital.

Traditional onchain trading makes positions, activity and strategy behavior visible by default. VeilVault explores a different model: confidential strategy execution combined with verifiable outcomes.

The initial build is focused on proving the core mechanism with a small number of controlled workflows before expanding toward a broader marketplace of private strategies.
